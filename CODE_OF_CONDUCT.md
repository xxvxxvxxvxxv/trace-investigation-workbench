# Code of Conduct

TRACE contributors and maintainers should provide a respectful, professional environment for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, discuss technical tradeoffs in good faith, accept corrections, credit others' work, and respect privacy. Use fictional examples in public discussions. Keep disagreements focused on ideas and behavior rather than personal characteristics.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publication of private investigation data, intimidation, and retaliation against good-faith reports are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict contribution privileges, or ban participants where necessary. Enforcement should be proportionate and protect affected people's privacy.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private channel. If none is configured, request a private contact method in a minimal issue without naming affected individuals or publishing sensitive details. Maintainers must not disclose a reporter's identity without consent unless required by applicable law.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and community spaces representing TRACE.
